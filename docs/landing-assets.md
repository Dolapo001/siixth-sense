# Landing page: assets and placeholders

The landing page (`/`) uses **original, temporary artwork** and **clearly labelled example content**. This lists what to replace before a public launch.

## Images

All image slots live in `src/features/landing/fixtures.ts` (`landingImages`). To replace one, drop a licensed file in `public/landing/` and change its `src` (and `width`/`height`/`alt`). Raster files (WebP/AVIF/JPEG) are optimised automatically by `next/image`; the current SVGs are served as-is.

| Slot | Current file | Used in | Replace with |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | `hero-coast-sunset.svg` | Hero background (eager-loaded) | Licensed or commissioned wide coastal city at sunset/night, ≥2400px wide, calm area on the left for text |
| `boulevard` | `boulevard-dusk.svg` | Hero preview, "One screenshot" scanner, discovery card | **Keep or redraw carefully:** the demo detection boxes (`demoDetections`) are positioned on its car, tower and flamingo. A replacement image needs new box coordinates. |
| `neonStreet` | `neon-street-night.svg` | Discovery cards | Night street with neon and a vehicle |
| `marsh` | `marsh-dusk.svg` | Discovery card, community demo | Wetland wildlife scene. The community demo box is positioned on the nearer flamingo (`community-preview.tsx`). |
| `pier` | `pier-sunset.svg` | Discovery card | Waterfront / pier |
| `skylineNight` | `skyline-night.svg` | Discovery card, final call to action | Night skyline |

The SVGs are generated by `scripts/generate-landing-art.mjs` (fictional scenes drawn in code, not taken from any game). Re-run it after editing: `node scripts/generate-landing-art.mjs`.

**Never use** Rockstar screenshots, GTA VI promotional material, logos, characters or maps unless Sixth Sense has written permission.

## Example content

Everything in `fixtures.ts` is demonstration content, labelled "Illustration" or "Example" in the UI:

- Demo detections and their confidence values (not model output).
- Example discoveries, with locations marked "(example)".
- The illustrative map: a fictional region, labelled "not the game map".
- The community verification walkthrough and the example attestation (signature reads "NOT A REAL TRANSACTION").

There are **no statistics or live-activity claims** on the page. If real ones are added later, they must come from the backend.

## Missing pages and links (not invented)

The brief's footer lists these, but no pages exist yet, so they're **left out** rather than linked to nothing:

- About, Contact, Privacy policy, Terms of service
- Social links (X, Discord, GitHub)

Add them to `footerColumns` in `src/components/landing/closing-sections.tsx` once the pages or accounts exist. Privacy and Terms should be in place before public launch.

## Typography

The page uses Sora (display and body) and JetBrains Mono (technical labels), the same fonts as the rest of the app, so the marketing site and product match.
